Balut Rules

Objective

The objective of the game is to earn the most points by rolling five dice to make certain combinations.

Gameplay

The player has 28 turns to score. In each turn, the dice can be rolled up to three times. On the first roll, the player rolls all dice. Then the player may keep any dice by clicking on them and reroll the remaining dice. The player is not required to roll dice for exactly three times. After the third (or earlier) roll, the player must select one scoring category on the scorecard.

There are totally 7 scoring categories, and each category can be used 4 times. If the player can't enter a score, he must enter a zero.

Scoring

The scorecard contains following categories:

Category Combination Score
Fours Dice with number four Total of fours
Fives Dice with number five Total of fives
Sixes Dice with number six Total of sixes
Straight Five sequential dice
(1,2,3,4,5 or 2,3,4,5,6)		 Sum of all dice
Full House Three-of-a-kind and a pair Sum of all dice
Choice Any combination Sum of all dice
Balut All five dice of the same number 20 plus sum of all dice

Points

The player gets points based on his score in each category as follows:

Category Requirement Points
Fours 52 or more 2
Fives 65 or more 2
Sixes 78 or more 2
Straight All Straights 4
Full House All Full Houses 3
Choice 100 or more 2
Balut For each Balut 2
