Crag Rules

Gameplay

The basic rules of Crag are similar to Yahtzee, but two dice are used. In each turn, the dice can be rolled up to two times. There are totally 13 scoring categories, and scoring rules are somewhat different.

Scoring

Category Combination Score
Ones Dice with number one Total of ones
Twos Dice with number two Total of twos
Threes Dice with number three Total of threes
Fours Dice with number four Total of fours
Fives Dice with number five Total of fives
Sixes Dice with number six Total of sixes
Low Straight 1,2,3 20
High Straight 4,5,6 20
Odd Straight 1,3,5 20
Even Straight 2,4,6 20
Three of a kind Three dice of the same number 25
Thirteen Any combination totalling 13 26
Crag Any combination with a pair and totalling 13 50
