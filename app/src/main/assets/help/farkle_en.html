Farkle Rules

Objective

The objective of the game is to score 10000 points to win.

Gameplay

The game is played with six dice. At the beginning each player rolls all dice. After each roll, the player is required to set aside at least one scoring dice. Then the player has to decide to either bank the accumulated current turn score, or continue rolling the remaining dice.

If a player has scored all six dice (Hot Dice), he again rolls six dice and continue accumulating scores for the turn. The player must have at least one scoring dice after each roll, otherwise he Farkle and loses all scores accumulated for this turn. It doesn't affect scores accumulated in previous turns.

Once a player has decided to bank or has Farkled, the turn is passed to the next player, who plays according to the same rules.

The first player to score 10000 points wins the game.

Scoring

Combination Score
Each One 100
Each Five 50
Three Ones 1000
Three Twos 200
Three Threes 300
Three Fours 400
Three Fives 500
Three Sixes 600
Four of a kind 2 times score of Three of a kind
Five of a kind 3 times score of Three of a kind
Six of a kind 4 times score of Three of a kind
Three pairs 750
Straight (1,2,3,4,5,6) 1500

Scores are only calculated for dice being set aside in the last roll. It doesn't count dice which were already set aside and scored in previous rolls. For example, if a player gets two Ones and sets them aside, scoring 200. In the next roll he gets another One and sets it aside, then it will only score 100, not 1000 for three Ones, so the total score would be 300.
