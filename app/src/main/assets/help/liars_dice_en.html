Liar's Dice Rules

Objective

2-4 players each roll 5 dice and can only see their own dice. The game is fixed at 10 rounds. At the end, players are ranked by the most rounds won, then the fewest rounds lost.

Gameplay

At the beginning of each round, all players roll their dice at the same time (the computer rolls automatically in this game). Players take turns in seat order making a bid, guessing the total count of a certain face value among all dice on the table, for example "3 x 5 Fei" means "I think there are at least three 5s among everyone's dice".

The bid format is "X of face Y Fei/Zhai":

Fei (default): 1s are wild and count as any number.

Zhai: 1s are not wild. For example "3 x 5 Zhai" means there must actually be three 5s without using 1s.

Calling 1: always uses Zhai mode.

Minimum bid (face 1 / Zhai / Fei): 2 players: 2/2/3; 3 players: 3/4/5; 4 players: 4/5/6.

Raising

On your turn you must bid higher than the previous bid:

Same mode (keeping Fei/Zhai): either a higher quantity (any face), or the same quantity with a higher face (face 1 is the highest). The maximum quantity is the total number of dice on the table (players × 5).

Zhai to Fei: the quantity must be at least 2 higher. E.g. after "3 x 3 Zhai" you must call at least "5 x X Fei".

Fei to Zhai: the quantity may be 1 lower. E.g. after "6 x 3 Fei" you can call "5 x X Zhai".

Revealing

If you suspect the previous bidder is bluffing, you can call "Liar!", and all dice are revealed. If the actual count ≥ the bid quantity, the challenger loses; otherwise the previous bidder loses.

The loser receives a drinking penalty and gets 1 loss; all other players get 1 win. The loser starts the next round. The game is fixed at 10 rounds, and at the end players are ranked by the most rounds won, then the fewest rounds lost.

Example

A 2-player game, each with 5 dice:

Player A: "two 4s Fei" (conservative start)

Player B: "three 4s Fei" (raise the quantity)

Player A: "three 5s Fei" (same quantity, higher face)

Player B: "Liar!" (challenge)

Result: reveal all dice and count. If there are indeed at least three 5s, Player B loses; if there are fewer than three 5s, Player A loses.
