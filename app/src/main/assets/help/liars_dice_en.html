Liar's Dice Rules

Gameplay

2-4 players each roll 5 dice and can only see their own dice. Players take turns to make a bid, guessing the total count of a certain face value among all dice of all players. For example, "3 x 5" means there are at least three 5s among everyone's dice.

Bid format

"X x Y Fei/Zhai"

  • Fei (default): 1s are wild and count as any number. For example, "3 x 5 Fei" means there are at least three 1s or 5s.
  • Zhai: 1s are not wild. For example, "3 x 5 Zhai" means there are at least three 5s without using 1s.
  • Calling 1: always uses Zhai mode.

Minimum bid

The minumun quantity of the first player's bid in each round (calling 1 / Zhai / Fei):

  • 2 players: 2/2/3
  • 3 players: 3/4/5
  • 4 players: 4/5/6

Raising

Each player must bid higher than the previous one:

  • Same mode (keeping Fei/Zhai): either a higher quantity (any face), or the same quantity with a higher face (face 1 is the highest).
  • Zhai to Fei: the quantity must be at least 2 higher. E.g. after "3 x 3 Zhai" you must call at least "5 x X Fei".
  • Fei to Zhai: the quantity may be 1 lower. E.g. after "6 x 3 Fei" you can call "5 x X Zhai".

The maximum quantity is the total number of dice of all players, that is players × 5.

Revealing

If you suspect the previous bidder is bluffing, you can call "Liar!", and all dice are revealed. If the bid is true (actual count ≥ bid quantity), the challenger loses; otherwise the bidder loses.

The winner gets 1 win; the loser receives a penalty and gets 1 loss; all other players don't change. The loser starts the next round. The game is fixed at 10 rounds, and at the end players are ranked by the most rounds won, then the fewest rounds lost.

Example

A 2-player game, each with 5 dice:

Player A: "3 x 4 Fei"

Player B: "4 x 4 Fei" (raise the quantity)

Player A: "4 x 5 Fei" (same quantity, higher face)

Player B: "Liar!" (challenge)

Result: reveal all dice. If there are indeed at least four 5s, Player B loses; if there are fewer than four 5s, Player A loses.
