Maxi Yatzy Rules

Gameplay

The basic rules are the same as Yahtzee except that six dice are used and there are totally 20 scoring categories, and scoring rules are somewhat different.

Scoring

The scorecard is divided into upper and lower sections.

Upper Section

Category Combination Score
Ones Dice with number one Total of ones
Twos Dice with number two Total of twos
Threes Dice with number three Total of threes
Fours Dice with number four Total of fours
Fives Dice with number five Total of fives
Sixes Dice with number six Total of sixes

Bonus

If the player scores a total of 84 or more in Upper Section, he can get 100 points bonus. ("84" is based on getting four of each number on average)

Lower Section

Category Combination Score
One pair A pair of dice of the same numbers Sum of the largest pair
Two pairs Two pairs of dice of the same numbers Sum of the largest two pairs
Three pairs Three pairs of dice of the same numbers Sum of the three pairs
Three of a kind Three dice of the same number Sum of the three dice
Four of a kind Four dice of the same number Sum of the four dice
Five of a kind Five dice of the same number Sum of the five dice
Small Straight 1,2,3,4,5 15
Large Straight 2,3,4,5,6 20
Full Straight 1,2,3,4,5,6 21
Full House Three dice of a number and two of another Sum of the five dice
Castle Three dice of a number and three of another Sum of all dice
Tower Four dice of a number and two of another Sum of all dice
Chance Any combination Sum of all dice
Maxi Yatzy All six dice of the same number 100
