Pig Rules

Objective

The objective of the game is to score 100 points to win.

Gameplay

The game is played with a single die. In each turn, the player can roll the die any number of times. After each roll, the die value is added to the turn score. Then the player can decide whether to chaim the turn score (hold) or continue rolling.

If a player rolls a 1 (Pig), he loses all scores for this turn. This has no impact on his overall score.

Once a player has decided to hold or has rolled a Pig, the turn is passed to the next player, who plays according to the same rules.

The first player to score 100 points wins the game.
