Yahtzee Rules

Objective

The objective of the Yahtzee game is to score the most points by rolling five dice to make certain combinations.

Gameplay

The player has 13 turns to score. In each turn, the dice can be rolled up to three times. In the first roll, the player rolls all dice. Then the player may keep any dice by clicking on them and reroll the remaining dice. The player is not required to roll dice for exactly three times. After the third (or earlier) roll, the player must select one scoring category on the scorecard.

There are totally 13 scoring categories, and each category can be used only once. If the player can't enter a score, he must enter a zero. The game ends when all categories are used. Scores are added up, including any bonus points.

Scoring

The scorecard is divided into upper and lower sections.

Upper Section

Category Combination Score
Ones Dice with number one Total of ones
Twos Dice with number two Total of twos
Threes Dice with number three Total of threes
Fours Dice with number four Total of fours
Fives Dice with number five Total of fives
Sixes Dice with number six Total of sixes

Bonus

If the player scores a total of 63 or more in Upper Section, he can get 35 points bonus. ("63" means getting three of each number on average)

Lower Section

Category Combination Score
Three of a kind At least three dice of the same number Sum of all dice
Four of a kind At least four dice of the same number Sum of all dice
Full House Three-of-a-kind and a pair 25
Small Straight Four sequential dice
(1,2,3,4 or 2,3,4,5 or 3,4,5,6)		 30
Large Straight Five sequential dice
(1,2,3,4,5 or 2,3,4,5,6)		 40
Chance Any combination Sum of all dice
Yahtzee All five dice of the same number 50

Joker Rule

If the player rolls a Yahtzee, while "Yahtzee" and the corresponding category in Upper Section have already been used, then he can score in any open categories in Lower Section, although this doesn't meet the normal requirement of these categories.

For example, if you roll five Fours, and you have used the "Fours" and "Yahtzee" categories, then you can score 40 points in "Large Straight".
